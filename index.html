OB-Xd for Mac

OB-Xd 3.20 is a digital synthesizer meticulously emulating the iconic Oberheim OB-X. It delivers the original's rich, full-bodied tones with enhanced features like micro random detuning for unparalleled depth. Ideal for electronic production, live recording, or film scoring, OB-Xd brings vintage character to modern workflows.

Second LFO (LFO2)

Independent second LFO runs parallel to LFO1, with dedicated rate, waveforms, amount controls, five destinations, and per-LFO tempo sync. Edit switch toggles between LFOs; old presets remain compatible with LFO2 silent.

MPE Support

Full MPE compatibility with per-axis routing for pitch bend, aftertouch, and timbre (CC74). Configurable depth, polarity, and destinations per axis. Timbre resets on note-off; settings persist across instances via MIDI.xml.

Advanced MIDI Learn & Boards

Reworked MIDI Learn with state-machine workflow, live LCD feedback, and reliable touch detection. LFO2 fully learnable. Manage custom MIDI boards: save, rename, delete, import/export. Auto-grouped by manufacturer, powered by MIDI.xml.

Knob LED Visualizer

Intuitive LED feedback: volume VU meter, filter cutoff tracking, ADSR animation, LFO modulation preview, velocity and envelope previews. Pan activity display, bipolar modes, and routing button flashes for precise control.

Workflow Enhancements

Persistent voicing preserves voice count, unison, and multiply settings across presets. Improved preset bar: anchored disk browser, native dialogs, bank management, search, and navigation shortcuts. Standalone MIDI Out via virtual CoreMIDI port.
